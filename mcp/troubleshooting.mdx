## Troubleshooting

### The connection says I do not have access

Sign in with an active Fundable account with a primary email. Email verification
and a paid subscription are not required. If connection setup or authorization
fails, reconnect to `https://mcp.tryfundable.ai/` or contact support. This differs
from a tool-level insufficient-credit response, which you can check with
`get_credit_balance`.

### A query returns too few results

Ask the assistant to inspect the dataset schema, validate exact filter values, and
run a small diagnostic query. Avoid guessing internal identifiers or category names.

### The assistant will not export or unlock a contact

Check your balance with `get_credit_balance` and buy credits on
[Fundable Credits](https://www.tryfundable.ai/credits). Active trials can use their
normal balance. State which file or contacts you want and approve the maximum
credits.

For help connecting Fundable MCP, contact [jacob@tryfundable.ai](mailto:jacob@tryfundable.ai).
